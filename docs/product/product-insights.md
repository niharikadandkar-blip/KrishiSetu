# Product Insights & Strategic Direction

## Core Insights for KrishiSetu

1. **Adaptation Over Instruction**:
   > *"Don't make the farmer learn KrishiSetu. Make KrishiSetu adapt to the farmer."*
   The UX must rely on familiar visual cues (crop images, large touch buttons, voice audio) rather than long paragraphs of text.

2. **Trust is a Layered Foundation**:
   Registration gets a user into the house; verification unlocks high-trust rooms. Never force complex verification before letting a farmer see market prices and explore the platform.

3. **Contextual Intelligence over Raw Numbers**:
   Showing a farmer that onion is selling for ₹2,500/q is useful. Showing them that Mandi A gives ₹2,500 but Mandi B (10km away) gives ₹2,600—and deducting transport costs shows Mandi A is actually more profitable—is actionable intelligence.

4. **Evidence-Based AI**:
   AI must never issue fake deterministic guarantees (e.g. "Price will definitely rise 15%"). It should provide probabilistic insights with explicit supporting evidence, data timestamps, and clear disclaimer of uncertainty.

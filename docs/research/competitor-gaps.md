# Competitor Gaps & Market Opportunities

## Core Unsolved Industry Gaps

### 1. From "Raw Mandi Prices" to "Net Realization Decision Support"
- **Existing State**: Apps display lists of mandi prices (e.g., *Mandi A: ₹2,400/q*, *Mandi B: ₹2,600/q*).
- **The Gap**: Farmers cannot easily calculate the **Net Realization** after factoring in:
  - Transport distance & fuel/freight cost
  - Mandi commission & loading/unloading fees (Hamali)
  - Crop wastage during longer transit
- **Opportunity**: Provide a **Net Realization Calculator** comparing nearby selling options to show estimated net cash in hand.

### 2. Multi-Level Trust & Verification Transparency
- **Existing State**: Platforms show generic green checkmarks or "Verified User" badges.
- **The Gap**: Users don't know *what* was verified, leading to misplaced trust during payments or quality disputes.
- **Opportunity**: Explicit multi-level badges:
  - Level 1: `Mobile Verified`
  - Level 2: `Profile / Identity Verified (DigiLocker)`
  - Level 3: `Marketplace / Listing Verified`

### 3. Integrated Regional Experience (Local Language + Low Literacy UX)
- **Existing State**: Apps offer English-to-Hindi translations that break on complex forms or display English technical jargon.
- **The Gap**: Low-literacy farmers are forced to navigate text-dense drop-down menus.
- **Opportunity**: Visual-first UX with large touch cards, agricultural iconography, audio prompts, and 100% complete localization in Marathi/Hindi/English.

### 4. Non-Blocking Onboarding
- **Existing State**: Apps block user entry until 15-20 profile fields, bank details, and identity documents are completed.
- **The Gap**: High initial drop-off before the farmer can explore app value.
- **Opportunity**: **Canonical Minimal Onboarding**: Landing → Language → Role → Registration → OTP → Location → **APP ACCESS**, allowing profile completion later inside the app.

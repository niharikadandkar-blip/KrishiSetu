# User Complaints, Praise, and Behavioral Patterns

## Farmer Feedback & Complaints

### 1. UX & Language Frustrations
- **English/Technical Jargon Overload**: Apps claiming regional language support often display English technical terms (e.g., "Assaying", "Moisture Content %", "Market Depth") translated literally or left un-translated.
- **Excessive Typing Requirements**: Forms requiring manual text entry of village names, crop varieties, or bank account details cause drop-offs.
- **Complex Hierarchies**: Multi-level drop-down lists (Category → Sub-category → Crop → Variety → Grade) frustrate users with limited digital experience.

### 2. Market Information & Price Gaps
- **"The price on the app is not what the trader gives me"**: Farmers complain that app prices reflect average mandi figures, but when they arrive at the mandi, quality deductions or local trader cartels offer significantly lower prices.
- **Lack of Net Realization Context**: Mandi price lists fail to account for transportation cost, loading/unloading fees (Hamali), commission fees, and wastage. A higher price 50km away often results in lower net income than selling locally.

### 3. Trust & Buyer Verification
- **Unverified Buyer Inquiries**: Farmers fear non-payment, payment delays, or quality rejections after shipping produce to unfamiliar buyers.
- **Ambiguous Verified Badges**: Apps displaying generic "Verified" icons without explaining *what* was verified (Mobile vs Identity vs Bank vs Physical Warehouse) create confusion and breach trust when disputes arise.

## Buyer & Trader Feedback & Complaints

### 1. Supply Quality & Standard Consistency
- **Non-Standardized Quality**: Buyers report that crop listings lack standardized moisture, size, and grade measurements, making distant purchases risky.
- **Listing Stale Data**: Buyers complain that farmer listings remain active long after the crop has already been sold.

### 2. Logistics & Delivery Coordination
- **Fragmented Logistics**: Arranging pickup from smallholder farms with poor road connectivity requires manual phone negotiation.

---

## What Users Genuinely Praise

1. **Visual Simplicity & Image-Based Communication**: Farmers love camera-based crop identification and large visual crop cards over text lists.
2. **Local Language Voice Audio**: Audio prompts explaining price trends or instructions in local dialects (e.g., Marathi / Deshi Marathi) receive high praise.
3. **Direct Contact with Nearby Buyers**: Quick phone/WhatsApp connect buttons for nearby verified traders build immediate confidence.
4. **Transparent Price Sources**: Showing explicit timestamping (e.g., *"Price recorded today at 10:30 AM at Niphad APMC"*) increases confidence.
